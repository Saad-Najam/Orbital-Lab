# Educational Experience

Status: design proposal. Learning-science citations not yet gathered. [U]

## Principles (to cite)
Learning by constructing and by comparing configurations; immediate feedback; worked examples before open design; errors as teachable moments. Cite educational-research sources (e.g. engineering-education literature) before claiming effectiveness.

## Journey
Intro -> architecture tour -> component inspection -> guided assembly -> subsystem labs (aero, propulsion, avionics, structures, thermal) -> integration check -> verification tests -> mission design -> flight and review.

## Activity types (no long text walls)
3D inspection with callouts; exploded/cutaway toggles; manipulable diagrams; slider experiments with live plots; wiring puzzles; "find the fault" scenarios; short knowledge checks tied to what the student just did; mini-projects (e.g. reach a 200 km orbit with a 2 t payload under a mass limit).

## Modules
1 Intro to rocket science; 2 Architecture; 3 Propulsion fundamentals; 4 Avionics and electronics; 5 Aerodynamics; 6 Structures; 7 Thermal; 8 Systems integration; 9 Flight dynamics; 10 Mission design.
Each: lessons, 3D demo, experiment, knowledge check, mini-project, completion tracking.
