# Reference Vehicles

Use: educational comparison of architecture only. **No exact internals claimed.** Figures below are what search returned on 2026-11-12; tag shows the source quality. Replace [S] items with primary manufacturer/agency documents before display.

## Comparison themes for teaching
Staging count and why; strap-on solid boosters (SLS, Ariane 6) vs liquid strap-ons (Soyuz) vs none (Falcon 9, Electron, New Glenn); reusability (Falcon 9, New Glenn, Electron partial); engine type (gas generator vs electric pump vs staged combustion at a very high level, only where publicly documented); payload class.
